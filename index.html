1. Overview & Introduction

Welcome to Strawberry Crossmath (“we”, “our”, or “the App”). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed when you download, install, and play our mathematical crossword puzzle game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected by third-party service providers, how it is handled, and the choices available to you. Strawberry Crossmath is designed to be played seamlessly offline and online without creating an account or providing any personally identifiable information.

Key Privacy Commitment: Strawberry Crossmath does NOT collect or store your personal name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts, financial info, or biometric data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To preserve your privacy and simplicity:

  • No User Account or Login: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or link any social media profile to play.
  • No Sensitive Personal Information: We do not collect names, government IDs, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Financial Data: We do not process or store credit card numbers, bank information, or billing addresses.
  • No Access to Device Sensors / Media: The App does not request camera, microphone, photo gallery, or device contact permissions.
  • Local Storage of Progress: Your level completions, stars, hints, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device (via secure local device storage) and are never sent to our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

Strawberry Crossmath is free to play and is supported by non-intrusive advertisements. We integrate the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded hint advertisements.

When you play the game while connected to the internet, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process certain device and diagnostic information to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or ID for Advertising, which allows ad networks to recognize your device for ad delivery, attribution, and frequency capping.
  • IP Address & Approximate Location: Your Internet Protocol (IP) address is used to determine general, coarse geographical location (country or city level) to serve regionally relevant ads and detect fraud. Precise GPS location is NEVER requested or collected.
  • Interaction & Diagnostic Logs: Technical metrics such as ad impressions, click events, app crash logs, launch timestamps, and performance diagnostics.

To learn more about how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review Google’s official policies:

🔗 Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy
🔗 How Google Uses Information From Apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Children’s Privacy & COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance

Strawberry Crossmath is an educational, fun math crossword puzzle game suitable for all ages. Protecting the privacy of young players is of paramount importance to us.

  • No Knowing Collection: We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions governed by the European Union General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR-K).
  • Google Play Families Policy: All advertisements delivered within Strawberry Crossmath comply with Google Play Families Policy standards. When configured for family audiences, ads served are family-safe, non-personalized, and do not track children for behavioral targeting.
  • Parental Inquiries: If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that personal information has been unintentionally collected, please contact us at support@strawberrycrossmath.com. We will promptly take steps to delete any such information.

6. Data Security

We prioritize the security and integrity of user interactions:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communications handled by third-party advertising SDKs are transmitted using strong HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+).
  • Isolated Local Storage: Game progress data (such as puzzle state, stars, and unlocked levels) is strictly isolated within the Android application sandbox on your physical device.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because we do not operate remote user account servers, we do not retain identifiable personal records on developer databases.

Your Deletion Rights:

  • Clear Game Data Locally: You can completely erase all local game data, level progress, and preferences at any time by going to your Android device’s Settings > Apps > Strawberry Crossmath > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Request Assistance: You may submit any data privacy or deletion inquiry to us at support@strawberrycrossmath.com, and we will fulfill your request promptly.

8. How to Reset or Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

Android gives you full native control over your Google Advertising ID (AAID) and personalized ads. If you prefer not to receive personalized advertisements:

  1. Open your Android device’s Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and select Google (or Privacy on newer versions of Android).
  3. Tap on Ads (or Ad Privacy).
  4. Select Reset advertising ID to unlink past browsing activity from your current ID.
  5. Select Delete advertising ID to prevent apps from accessing an advertising identifier altogether.
  6. Alternatively, enable Opt out of Ads Personalization (where available).

Even after opting out or deleting your advertising ID, you may still see non-personalized ads based on the general context of the game.
